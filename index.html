How to Play:
  • Each level shows a green goal at (x, y) on both panels
  • Enter a function f(x) in Desmos with a removable discontinuity (hole) at the goal's x-coordinate
  • The limit of f(x) as x approaches the hole must equal the goal's y-coordinate
  • Click Validate to check your function, then Start to drop the ball
  • The ball follows the curve through the hole — reach the goal within 6 seconds!
